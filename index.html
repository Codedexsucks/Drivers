Each car reads five distance sensors, its forward speed, and its sideways slip. Its network outputs steering and a signed throttle (negative = brake).

Grip falls as speed and steering demand rise. A car that enters a corner too hot keeps sideways velocity and drifts wide. Forward speed under 2 for 3 seconds kills it.
